Glossary

Common statistical and transit terms used throughout the analyses.

Transit Metrics

OTP (OTP)

On-time performance, represented as a fraction between 0 and 1.

Ridership-Weighted OTP

OTP averaged using passenger counts as weights.

Statistical Methods

Pearson Correlation

Linear association statistic ranging from -1 to 1.

Spearman Correlation

Rank-based monotonic association statistic.
