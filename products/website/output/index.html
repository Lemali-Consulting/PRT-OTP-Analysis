Generated documentation site for pipeline and analyses.

Pipeline

  • Data Ingestion - Builds the normalized SQLite database from canonical local CSV sources.

Analyses

  • 01 - System-Wide OTP Trend - Tracks the overall PRT on-time performance trend from 2019 through 2025, including COVID impact and recovery. (Core OTP Patterns)
  • 02 - Mode Comparison - Compares on-time performance across service modes (BUS, RAIL, INCLINE) and route types (local, limited, express, busway). (Core OTP Patterns)
  • 03 - Route Ranking - Ranks routes by average OTP, trend direction, and volatility to identify best/worst performers and most (in)consistent routes. (Core OTP Patterns)
  • 04 - Neighborhood Equity - Investigates whether on-time performance varies systematically by neighborhood and municipality. (Core OTP Patterns)
  • 05 - Anomaly Investigation - Identifies and investigates sharp OTP drops that may indicate route restructuring, detours, or data quality issues. (Core OTP Patterns)
  • 06: Seasonal Patterns - Decomposes route-level OTP into trend, seasonal, and residual components to identify whether summer or winter months systematically affect performance. (Core OTP Patterns)
  • 07: Stop Count vs OTP - Tests whether routes with more stops have worse on-time performance, using a scatter plot of stop count against average OTP with mode-based coloring. (Core OTP Patterns)
  • 08: Hot-Spot Map - Visualizes stop-level on-time performance on a geographic scatter plot to identify corridor-level bottlenecks and clusters of poor performance. (Core OTP Patterns)
  • 09: Incline Investigation - Audits the Monongahela Incline data across all database tables to determine why it appears in OTP data with zero/null values. (Core OTP Patterns)
  • 10: Trip Frequency vs OTP - Tests whether high-frequency routes have worse on-time performance, using weekday trip counts as a proxy for service frequency. (Route and Service Drivers)
  • 11: Directional Asymmetry - Investigates whether routes with a structural imbalance between inbound and outbound trip frequency have worse on-time performance. (Route and Service Drivers)
  • 12: Route Geographic Span vs OTP - Computes the geographic span (max distance between any two stops) for each route and tests whether longer routes have worse on-time performance, disentangling route length from stop count. (Route and Service Drivers)
  • 13: Cross-Route Correlation Clustering - Computes pairwise OTP time-series correlations between all routes and uses hierarchical clustering to identify groups of routes whose performance rises and falls together. (Route and Service Drivers)
  • 14: COVID Recovery Trajectories - Measures how far each route's OTP has recovered relative to its pre-COVID baseline and identifies route characteristics that predict faster or slower recovery. (Route and Service Drivers)
  • 15: Municipal/County Equity - Aggregates on-time performance by municipality and county to assess service reliability equity at a broader geographic level than neighborhood analysis (Analysis 04). (Route and Service Drivers)
  • 16: Transfer Hub Performance - Identifies high-connectivity stops (served by many routes) and tests whether passengers at transfer hubs experience worse OTP than those at low-connectivity stops. (Route and Service Drivers)
  • 17: Weekend vs Weekday Service Profile - Tests whether routes with different weekend-to-weekday service ratios show different OTP patterns, distinguishing commuter-oriented routes from all-day service routes. (Route and Service Drivers)
  • 18: Multivariate OTP Model - Combines stop count, mode, bus subtype, geographic span, and service profile into a single OLS regression model to quantify relative importance and total explained variance. (Route and Service Drivers)
  • 19 - Ridership-Weighted OTP - Compute system OTP weighted by actual average daily ridership instead of scheduled trip frequency, to measure the average rider's experience. (Route and Service Drivers)
  • 20 - OTP → Ridership Causality - Test whether OTP declines predict subsequent ridership losses using lagged correlation and Granger causality tests. (Ridership and External Factors)
  • 21 - COVID Ridership vs OTP Recovery - Compare ridership recovery trajectories with OTP recovery trajectories post-COVID to identify whether ridership recovery degrades OTP. (Ridership and External Factors)
  • 22 - Passenger-Weighted Delay Burden - Estimate late rider-trips per route per month by combining ridership with OTP to identify where the most total human impact occurs. (Ridership and External Factors)
  • 23 - Garage-Level Performance - Compare OTP and ridership trends across PRT garages (Ross, Collier, East Liberty, West Mifflin) to surface operational differences. (Ridership and External Factors)
  • 24 - Weekday vs Weekend Ridership Trends - Track how weekday, Saturday, and Sunday ridership patterns shifted post-COVID and whether weekend ridership share correlates with OTP. (Ridership and External Factors)
  • 25 - Ridership Concentration & Equity - Measure what share of total system ridership is carried by the lowest-OTP routes using Lorenz curves and Gini coefficients. (Ridership and External Factors)
  • 26 - Ridership in Multivariate OTP Model - Add ridership as a predictor to the Analysis 18 OLS model to test whether it adds explanatory power beyond stop count, span, and mode. (Ridership and External Factors)
  • 27 - Traffic Congestion and OTP - Tests whether PennDOT AADT traffic volume explains OTP variance beyond structural features (Ridership and External Factors)
  • 28 - Weather Impact - Tests whether weather (precipitation, snow, temperature) explains OTP variance or the counterintuitive seasonal pattern from Analysis 06. (Ridership and External Factors)
  • 29 - Service Change Impact on OTP - Do schedule changes (pick period transitions) correlate with OTP shifts? (Ridership and External Factors)
  • 30 - Service Level vs OTP Longitudinal - Within-route panel: does changing trip frequency improve or degrade OTP? (Equity and Strategic Planning)
  • 31 - Stop Consolidation Candidates - Identify low-usage stops that could be consolidated to improve OTP, leveraging the finding that stop count is the strongest OTP predictor. (Equity and Strategic Planning)
  • 32 - Shelter Equity - Assess whether bus shelters are equitably placed relative to stop-level ridership volume and demographics. (Equity and Strategic Planning)
  • 33 - Pandemic Ridership Geography - Map the spatial pattern of stop-level ridership loss and recovery between pre-pandemic and pandemic periods. (Equity and Strategic Planning)
  • 34 - Ridership Concentration (Pareto) - Quantify how concentrated ridership is across stops and test whether concentration correlates with route OTP. (Equity and Strategic Planning)
  • 35 - Boarding/Alighting Flow Analysis - Analyze net boarding-alighting flows by stop and direction to identify major trip generators and attractors. (Equity and Strategic Planning)
  • 36 - National Ridership Growth (2019 vs 2024) - Compare 2019-to-2024 ridership recovery across the 150 largest US transit agencies using NTD data; rank PRT nationally. (Equity and Strategic Planning)
  • 37 - Peer City Ridership Comparison - Track indexed monthly ridership for Pittsburgh and 7 peer cities from 2019-2025 using NTD data; compare recovery trajectories and mode splits. (Equity and Strategic Planning)
