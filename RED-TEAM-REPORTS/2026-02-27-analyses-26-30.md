## Out of Scope

1. **Analysis 28, Block A regression variable selection (main.py:236):** The subset of weather variables used in the multiple regression (`reg_vars` at line 236) is chosen without formal variable selection criteria (e.g., stepwise, LASSO). With 9 candidate weather variables and n~72, overfitting risk is present even with the reduced 5-variable set. This is a modeling choice rather than a checklist violation.

2. **Analysis 29, causal interpretation:** The FINDINGS discusses schedule changes as associated with OTP shifts, but the design cannot distinguish whether schedule changes *cause* OTP improvements or are *timed to coincide with* expected improvements (selection bias). The FINDINGS caveats mention this, but the headline "statistically significant positive OTP shift" framing in the Summary may overstate the causal interpretation. This is a framing concern rather than a statistical error.

3. **Analysis 28, `save_model_comparison` unused parameter (main.py:657, 722):** The function signature accepts `block_a_reg` as a parameter but it is called with `None` (line 722) and the parameter is never used in the function body. This is dead code, not a correctness issue.

4. **Analyses 26 and 27, `n_munis` suppressor variable:** Both analyses inherit the `n_munis` suppressor identified in Analysis 18's red-team report. This was previously flagged and addressed in the 2026-02-10 report (finding #22), so it is not re-flagged here, though the suppressor behavior may differ on the restricted samples used in these analyses.
