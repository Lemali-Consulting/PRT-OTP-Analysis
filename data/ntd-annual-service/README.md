# NTD Annual Service Data (TS2.2)

FTA National Transit Database TS2.2 "Service Data and Operating Expenses Time-Series by System" workbook. Contains annual Vehicle Revenue Hours (VRH), Vehicle Revenue Miles (VRM), Unlinked Passenger Trips (UPT), and Vehicles Operated in Maximum Service (VOMS) for ~3,000 US transit agencies from 1991 to 2023.

**Source:** <https://www.transit.dot.gov/ntd/data-product/ts22-service-data-and-operating-expenses-time-series-system-0>
