# 53 - Near-Side vs. Far-Side Stop Placement

Classifies each PRT bus stop as near-side (before the traffic light), far-side (after it), or mid-block, and validates a from-scratch GTFS/OSM geometric heuristic against PRT's own authoritative stop records. 86% of stops at signalized intersections are near-side — the legacy default — vs. 14% far-side; the heuristic matched PRT's records on 98% of stops, and near/far placement has no measurable association with route OTP.
