# 50 - Bus vs. Rail: Productivity by Mode

Splits the agency-wide service productivity decline of Analysis 48 — passengers carried per hour of service operated — into PRT's four modes: motor bus, light rail, paratransit (the door-to-door ACCESS service), and the Monongahela Incline. Reports each mode's productivity from 2002 to 2024, how the post-2019 decline differed by mode, and how each mode's share of service hours compares with its share of riders.
